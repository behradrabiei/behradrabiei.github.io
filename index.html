Behrad Rabiei

I'm a PhD candidate at the University of Michigan, co-advised by Prof. Maani Ghaffari and Prof. Yulun Tian. I work on vision-language-action (VLA) models for robot navigation and mobile manipulation, with a focus on memory and scene representations.

Previously, I worked with Prof. Nikolay Atanasov at UC San Diego on language-grounded task planning, translating natural-language instructions into temporal logic that robots can plan and navigate with.

Research

I'm most interested in how robots remember: what a navigation policy should keep as it moves through an environment, and whether that memory is better held inside the model or in an explicit representation of the scene. I also study how to make VLA models more efficient and easier to interpret.
